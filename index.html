Controls

    Attack/special + a direction picks the move. Up + special is your recovery. Down on a floating platform drops through it. Hold down to crouch (shorter, and you fly less far when hit). Shield + left/right rolls, shield + down spot dodges, shield in the air air-dodges (hold a direction to dash about 3 m; you get one per jump; dodge down-and-sideways into the floor to wavedash). Parry (its own button) right before a hit lands and it bounces off: the attacker is stuck for a moment, and projectiles fly back at whoever threw them. Mistime it and you're open. Fighters can't walk through each other: you shove them along. Grab (or attack while shielding) grabs: then attack to headbutt, or a direction to throw. Mash buttons to break out of a grab. Fall next to the stage edge to grab the ledge, then press up/toward the stage to climb, jump to leap off, attack for a getup kick, shield to roll in, or down to let go. Tap attack again during a jab for a combo. Esc or Start pauses. The ` key shows hitboxes.
