Friend Group Brawl

Controls

    Attack/special + a direction picks the move. Up + special is your recovery. Down on a floating platform drops through it. Shield + left/right rolls, shield + down spot dodges, shield in the air air-dodges. Fall next to the stage edge to grab the ledge, then press up/toward the stage to climb, jump to leap off, attack for a getup kick, shield to roll in, or down to let go. Tap attack again during a jab for a combo. Esc or Start pauses. The ` key shows hitboxes.
